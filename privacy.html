Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Metronome Uploader is a private tool operated by Music Club. It is used only by the channel owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to organize them into playlists.

What the tool accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their visibility and publish time, and add them to playlists on the owner's channels. It reads the channel's own ID and title to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share personal information about anyone else.
  • It does not access viewers' data, comments or subscribers.
  • It does not sell or transfer any data to third parties.

Where access tokens are kept

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never published. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Metronome Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the About page of the Music Buddy channel.
